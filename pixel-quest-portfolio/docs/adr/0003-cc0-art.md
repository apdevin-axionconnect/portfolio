# ADR 0003: Use Kenney's CC0 art rather than custom or trademarked sprites

**Status:** accepted · 2026-11

## Context
The brief was "like a Mario game". Nintendo's sprites are trademarked and copyrighted, custom art is weeks of work,
and AI-generated pixel art looked generic in the first iteration.

## Decision
Use Kenney's *Platformer Art Complete Pack* (CC0 1.0, public domain): 70 px vector-style tiles, three alien heroes with
walk, jump and badge poses, critters, HUD icons and painted backdrops. Credit is kept in `src/assets/hd/CREDITS.txt`.

## Consequences
- Publishable and forkable without a licence question; credit is optional but given.
- Consistent art direction across four world themes with no drawing work.
- The look is recognisably "Kenney"; a custom hero sprite is the obvious upgrade if the portfolio needs a signature.
